Game Introduction
Welcome to the realm of ZENONIA 3 which contains 4 individual classes, over 2,500 costumes, 200 maps, detailed system and an epic scenario. You are now ready to set sail on a mystical journey with Chael, through the mysterious continent of Midgard.
Short Keys
Skills and items may be registered as a short key, and you can alternate between slot 1 and 2 by selecting the changing button.
Class

Sword Knight:Tenacious melee class wielding the finest sword.
Shadow Hunter: Agile melee class favoring swift attacks using both hands.
Mechanic Launcher: Fusion long-range class firing deadly mechanical weapons.
Nature Shaman: Mystical long-rang class who uses natural magic and totems.
Ability
HP: Indicates the health of character. Game will end when the HP value depletes to 0.
SP: Indicates the stamina of character. Value will deplete when skills are used.
EXP: Indicates the experience of character. Acquiring experience will increase the level of your character. It can be earned through completing quests and hunting monsters.
Individual classes have special stats that influence other abilities.
Strength: Affects attack and defense of character.
Dexterity: Affects accuracy, evasion, critical attack and critical evasion of character.
Condition: Affects total HP and recovery speed of character.
Intelligence: Affects total SP and recovery speed of character.
Status Info
ATTACK: : Displays character information of ATT (attack), HIT (accuracy) and CRI (critical attack).
DEFFENCE: Displays character information of DEF (defense), EVA (evade), C.EV (critical evasion).
STATE: Displays character information of Health (HP), Stamina (SP), Strength (STR), Dexterity (DEX), Condition (CON) and Intelligence (INT).
PVP: Displays information of PvP record.
Skill
Each individual class has unique skills.
Active: Categorized into attacking and buff skills which may be used by selecting a short key.
Passive: Continuous skill that increases the character’s basic ability.
Skill Point: Points which will increase the skill of character. They can be acquired after leveling up.
Item
Type: Categorized into Weapon, Armor, Accessory, Miscellaneous, and Sheathe Stone. Durability of Weapon and Armor items will wear out and require constant repair.
Class: Normal, Magic, Rare, Epic and Unique classes each possess different effects and values. They may be acquired by hunting monsters or through Dark Merchants.
Set: Eight set items exist per level. Each set comprises of Weapon, Helmet and Armor items. Equip a complete set to activate a special set effect.
Sheathe Stone: Sheathe Stone may be extracted by mixing two items of the same class.
Combine: Special items may be acquired by combining all ingredients of the Combine Book. All items produced through combining will be above magic class.
Refine: Refining will increase the strength of the item. Refining process may be repeated a maximum of 11 times, but will vary according to item class.
Strengthen: Transfer abilities of the Sheathe Stone by applying it to the item. There is no limitation on the number of applications, but previously applied abilities will be removed when a new Sheathe Stone is applied.
Examine: Unexamined items can be acquired through hunting monsters, but cannot be equipped. Unexamined items must be examined by using an Examine Scroll which may be bought from the Z SHOP or acquired randomly during play.
Unique Items
Unique items possess special option which does not exist in other classes of items.
Absorb HP/SP: Increase HP/SP by 10% of the damage inflicted on monsters.
Acquired Gold Amount: Increase Gold acquired after killing monsters.
Item Drop: Increase item drop rate.
Reflection: Reflect damaged received by a chance of 30%.
Movement Speed: Increase movement speed of character by one level.
Knockdown Chance: Increase knockdown chance according to the character’s basic attack.
System
Fairy
Runa always accompanies and helps Chael. Equip Rune or Skill Stones, which can be acquired through the Spirit Manager, to improve her status. Level of Fairy will determine the quality of the Stone. Please note that equipping a new Stone will nullify the effects of the previously equipped Stone.
Mining
Ores are found in certain maps, and they can be mined using a pickax. Gold, Ores and Gems will randomly drop from the Ores. However, mining will decrease the durability of the pickax and it will be destroyed when the durability depletes completely.
Title
Titles can be acquired for completing hidden missions. Acquired titles can be equipped to increase the ability of the character.
Legendary Monster
Minority of Legendary Monsters will randomly appear at certain maps. They are similar in appearance to normal monsters, but possess superior powers. Legendary Monsters will drop rare items at a higher rate and you will be rewarded with additional EXP and Gold for killing them.
Sheathe Stone, Combine and Refine
Sheathe Stone
Sheathe Stone can be acquired by mixing two items of the same class. Sheathe Stones may be applied to items to increase their attributes. However, applying a new Sheathe Stone will nullify the effects of the previous Sheathe Stone.
Combine
Create items at the Blacksmith by collecting and combining ingredients listed on a Combine Scroll. Please note that failed attempts will destroy all the ingredients.
Refine
Equipment items can be refined to increase their ability. Refining can be repeated, but the maximum number of refining will be determined by the class of the item.
Arena
Talk to the Arena Official and enter the PvP Arena to duel against other users. Check your PvP record and acquire special arena items according to your win points. Challenge yourself against other brave warriors of ZENONIA 3!
Execution Room
Talk to the Execution Room NPC and engage in a battle against monsters that appear at each floor. Play on your own or enjoy party play by registering your friend’s character. Nephrolith of Aid is required to register friend’s character that has a higher level character. Fast-paced battles will allow you to acquire EXP quickly, and you can acquire special items for clearing floors.
Quest
Main: Mandatory quests which must be completed for progression. Main quests cannot be quit.
Sub: Optional quests that can be progressed when special conditions have been met. Sub quests may be quit.
Status Effect
Flame: Receives constant flame damage.
Frost: Slow down character’s movement speed.
Holy: Momentarily stops all actions.
Darkness: Moves character in opposite direction.
Knockdown: Character falls more than the usual distance.
Reduce Attack: Reduces attacking power of character.
Reduce Defense: Reduces defensive power of character.
Reduce Elemental Resist: Reduces elemental resistance of character.
Slow: Slow down character’s movement speed.
Stun: Becomes in a volatile state.
Numb: Momentarily stops the character until he receives damage.
Bleeding: Receives constant bleeding damage.
Addiction: Receives constant addiction damage.
Confusion: Moves character in opposite direction.
Community
Ranking: Register and check your ranking.
Send and Receive Mail: Send and receive mail with items or gold attached to other users of ZENONIA 3. Maximum of 10 mails will be stored and excessive mails will be automatically deleted according to the received date.
Game Modes
ZENONIA 3 contains normal, hard and hell mode.
Acquire rare items and fight against stronger monsters in the hard and hell modes of ZENONIA 3!
